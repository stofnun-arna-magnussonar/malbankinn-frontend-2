Icelandic Terminology Database of the Translation Centre of the Ministry for Foreign Affairs

For three decades, the Translation Centre of the Ministry for Foreign Affairs has been translating legal texts falling under the Agreement on the European Economic Area (EEA Agreement). From the very beginning of this translation work, terms, and phrases have been collected in the Terminology Database. Most of the terms relate to the various subject areas of the EEA Agreement, including social rights, transport, finance, pharmaceuticals, consumer affairs and environmental matters. The database also contains many terms from legal language and public administration, as well as names of international agreements, institutions, committees, councils, and more. The vocabulary of the Icelandic International Development Agency has also been published in the database. The database now holds over 82,000 entries, each accompanied by several information fields. New terms are added on a regular basis.
