The Written Language Archive of the Institute of Lexicography

The Written Language Archive of the Institute of Lexicography contains citations from printed books and journals, and a number of manuscripts, from 1540 onwards. Almost all printed material from 1540 up to the 19th century has been excerpted, and also a great number of texts from the 19th and 20th centuries. The archive contains around 2.5 million citations exemplifying almost 700,000 lexemes.
