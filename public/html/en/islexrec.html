About the ISLEX recordings

Recordings of the pronunciation of each entry in the digital dictionaries published by The Árni Magnússon Institute. The list contains 49,000 audio files of individual headwords plus recordings of the pronunciation of 700 phrases. The recordings were made in September 2012 at studio Upptekið in Reykjavík. The reader is Guðfinna Rúnarsdóttir (born 1963). The recordings are available as OGG, MP3 and WAV files.
